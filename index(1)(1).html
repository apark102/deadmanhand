Dead Man's Hand

You lost your soul at the tables. The way out is nine floors up. Rooms lock until every enemy is down, the House guards each exit, and if you linger, the Collector comes for what you owe.

  • Every throw draws a card from your shoe and adds it to your total. Higher cards hit harder.
  • Throw two to fill the clip, then hit again or stand. Standing reloads.
  • Stand on 17 or more to power up your next hand and grow your streak. Every hand in the streak adds 15% more. Standing under 17 ends it. A two card 21 is a blackjack and sets off a shockwave.
  • Go over 21 and you bust: the gun jams and you lose health, more the longer your streak was.
